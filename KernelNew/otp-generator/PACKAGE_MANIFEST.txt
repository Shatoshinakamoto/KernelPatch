## Quick Start After Download

1. Extract the archive:
   tar -xzf otp-generator-complete.tar.gz
   cd otp-generator

2. Option A - Use pre-compiled binary:
   ./otp --help

3. Option B - Build from source:
   make
   ./otp --help

4. Generate your first OTP:
   ./otp -g myemail@gmail.com -i Google -m
